Core Concept

Two Wallets, No Illusions

Neraca's core idea: keep operating cash separate from future savings so your balance never lies.

Daily Cash

Operating Money

For everyday and monthly spending. Expenses cut this balance directly, and income adds to it.

Dream Savings

Protected Money

Emergency funds, investments, and future goals. Immune to daily spending — it only grows from transfers or allocated savings.

Key Features

Everything You Need to Save Tidily

From AI-powered quick notes to budget-limit alerts — all in a single app.

🤖

Acha AI Financial Copilot

Type "gas 25k, meatballs 15k, latte 20k" — it splits them into individual transactions. Scan receipts with your camera (OCR) or discuss your monthly cash flow.

📊

Real-Time Google Sheets Sync

Every transaction is written to your own spreadsheet automatically — two-way, with no manual export/import. Monthly tabs plus formula reconciliation.

🚨

Budget Limits & Alerts

Set monthly quotas for 7 expense categories. Get notified at 80% (warning) or 100% (overbudget) — with anti-spam protection.

🎯

Savings Goals

Set a target amount with a deadline. Neraca calculates the required monthly contribution and shows a progress bar with time remaining.

🛡️

Isolated Guest Mode

Try it without a Google account. Guest data is strictly guarded: Drive, Sheets, and background sync are fully blocked — zero data leakage.

🔄

Persistent Session

Stay signed in even after the app is force-closed. The session only ends when you press Sign Out — not when the app is backgrounded.
