1. Who We Are

Neraca ("the App") is a personal finance management application developed by Dalvero ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, stored, and shared, and what choices you have. It applies to the mobile application and this website.

The App is designed as a single-user, private tool: your financial records belong to you, and we do not sell, rent, or monetize your data in any way.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you sign in, we receive:

  • Google Sign-In: your email address, display name, and profile photo, as permitted by the Google OAuth consent screen.
  • Email & Password: your email address and an encrypted password (handled by Firebase Authentication; passwords are never visible to us).
  • User ID: an internal identifier (Firebase UID or a local guest identifier) used to partition your data on the device.

2.2 Financial Data

  • Transactions (income, expenses, transfers), wallet balances (Daily Cash and Dream Savings), category budgets, and savings goals.
  • This data is stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences, keyed by your user ID.
  • If you choose to enable Google Sheets sync, the same data is written to your own Google Spreadsheet (see Section 4). We never receive a copy on our servers — we have no servers for this purpose.

2.3 AI Content (Acha AI)

  • Text you type into the Acha AI assistant (for example, spending notes or questions about your finances).
  • Images you choose to process — photos of receipts or bank/transaction statements you scan with the camera or pick from your gallery.
  • These inputs are sent to the Google Gemini API for natural-language parsing and OCR. Chat history is stored locally on your device, keyed by your user ID.

2.4 Profile Photo

If you upload a profile picture, the image is uploaded to Cloudinary (unsigned upload) and its URL is stored in your local profile.

2.5 Data We Do Not Collect

  • No precise location, contacts, or microphone access.
  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No bank credentials — Neraca never asks for or stores your banking login.
  • Notification content is generated and displayed only on your device (budget alerts, reminders).

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the core features: recording transactions, calculating balances, transfers between wallets, and budget tracking.
  • To sync your ledger with your own Google Spreadsheet when you explicitly connect it.
  • To process the text and images you submit to Acha AI so it can parse transactions, read receipts, and answer financial questions.
  • To keep you signed in between sessions and restore your data when you switch accounts on the same device.
  • To display local notifications for budget warnings (80% / 100% thresholds) and savings-goal reminders.

Purpose limitation: your data is used only to run the App's features for you. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Local-first: financial data lives in device storage (SharedPreferences) and is partitioned by user ID (e.g., transactions are keyed per account), so multiple accounts or guests on one device never mix data.
  • Your spreadsheet, your cloud: if you enable sync, data goes directly from your device to your own Google account. We cannot read it.
  • Transport: all API traffic uses TLS/HTTPS.
  • Sessions: login state is stored locally on the device; sign out at any time to clear the active session.

6. Guest Mode

You can use Neraca without an account. In Guest Mode the App first attempts Firebase Anonymous Authentication; if offline, it falls back to a purely local guest session. Google Drive, Google Sheets, and all background sync are hard-blocked in Guest Mode — guest financial data never leaves the device.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You are in control of your data. You can:

  1. Delete your spreadsheet: because sync writes to your own Google Spreadsheet, you can edit or delete it directly in Google Sheets/Drive at any time.
  2. Clear local data: remove the App's data via Android Settings → Apps → Neraca → Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the App.
  3. Sign out: use the Logout button in the App to end your session and clear the active session state.
  4. Request account deletion: contact us at the address in Section 10 and we will delete your Firebase account and any data we can associate with it.

Where GDPR or similar law applies, you may also request access to, correction of, or a copy of your personal data, and you may object to or restrict certain processing.

8. Children's Privacy

Neraca is a personal finance tool intended for general audiences. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change when edits are made. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
